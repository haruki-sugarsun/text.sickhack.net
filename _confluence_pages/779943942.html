Ollama Model Directory Location

By default, Ollama stores its downloaded models in a directory within your user profile:

  • Linux/macOS: ~/.ollama/models or sometimes /usr/share/ollama/.ollama/models or /var/lib/ollama/.ollama/models depending on installation method and system configuration.

  • Windows: C:\Users\%username%\.ollama\models.
